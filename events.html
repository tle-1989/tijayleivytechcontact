Lafayette Campus Fitness Competition

Possible
Competition Events

Take a look at some of the strength, endurance, and fitness challenges being considered for the Lafayette Campus Fitness Competition.

These are examples of possible events. Final events, rules, weights, distances, and formats may change based on participation, available equipment, and safety.

Event 01

Farmer Carry

Participants carry a weighted dumbbell or other weighted implement in each hand while moving through a designated course.

Focus: Grip strength, total-body strength, and endurance

Possible Goal: Complete the designated distance in the fastest time.

Event 02

Tire Flip

Participants repeatedly flip a large tire forward across a designated distance.

Focus: Lower-body strength, upper-body strength, power, and conditioning

Possible Goal: Complete the designated distance in the fastest time.

Event 03

Sled Push

Participants push a weighted sled across a designated course as quickly as possible.

Focus: Leg strength, power, and cardiovascular endurance

Possible Goal: Push the sled across the course in the fastest time.

Event 04

Sled Pull

Participants use straps or handles to pull a weighted sled across a designated distance.

Focus: Lower-body strength, grip, endurance, and total-body conditioning

Possible Goal: Pull the sled across the course in the fastest time.

Event 05

Sandbag Carry

Participants carry a weighted sandbag over a designated distance while maintaining control of the weight.

Focus: Total-body strength, core stability, grip, and endurance

Possible Goal: Complete the designated course in the fastest time.

Event 06

Deadlift

Participants lift a loaded barbell from the ground to a standing position using proper deadlift technique.

Focus: Total-body strength and pulling strength

Possible Goal: Complete as many successful repetitions as possible within a designated time.

Event 07

Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Participants press a dumbbell overhead from shoulder level until the arm reaches the required finishing position.

Focus: Shoulder strength, upper-body strength, and muscular endurance

Possible Goal: Complete as many successful repetitions as possible within a designated time.

Event 08

Iron Cross Hold

Participants hold a dumbbell in each hand with their arms extended out to the sides at approximately shoulder height.

Focus: Shoulder strength, muscular endurance, and stability

Possible Goal: Maintain the required position for as long as possible.
