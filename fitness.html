The Competition

Strength.
Endurance.
Challenge.

This event would give participants an opportunity to test their strength, endurance, and overall fitness through a variety of fun and challenging events inspired by strongman competitions.

Events may include activities such as carries, pushes, pulls, lifts, holds, and other strength-based challenges.

The competition would primarily be geared toward students, but faculty and staff are also welcome to participate.

This survey is being used to determine overall interest, preferred scheduling, and the types of events participants would like to see.

Completing the survey does not commit you to participating.
