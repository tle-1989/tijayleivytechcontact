About

Educational technology with a practical, faculty-centered focus.

My work in educational technology is centered on helping faculty use digital tools effectively, confidently, and purposefully. I am especially interested in creating practical, sustainable approaches to technology-supported teaching and learning that strengthen the faculty and student experience.

Professional Experience

What I Do

I specialize in educational technology, instructional design, faculty development, and academic operations. As Lead Educational Technology Coordinator at Ivy Tech Community College, I lead technology-supported teaching and learning initiatives, develop faculty professional development, support Canvas and emerging technologies, and collaborate with academic leadership to improve the faculty and student experience.

I also build and lead programs and support systems from the ground up, including the Educational Technology and Audio/Video Support departments, and develop the training, documentation, workflows, and resources needed to sustain them.
