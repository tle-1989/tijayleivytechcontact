Maximum Heart Rate

Estimate your age-predicted maximum heart rate and exercise intensity zones.

YOUR RESULTS187 bpm
About this formula

Tanaka: 208 − (0.7 × age). Age-predicted maximum heart rate varies substantially between individuals. Not a medical exercise prescription.

MUSCULAR STRENGTH

One-Repetition Maximum

Estimate the most weight you could lift for one repetition.

ESTIMATED 1RM208.1 lb

Based on 185 lb × 5 repetitions

About this formula

Brzycki: 1RM = weight × 36 ÷ (37 − reps). Estimates are most useful when the set is performed near failure, particularly with lower repetition counts.
